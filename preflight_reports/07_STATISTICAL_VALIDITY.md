The historical Stage 18/22/24 analyses use shared endpoint-node draws within strata and matched weights for policy contrasts. Stage 18 regenerated all 5,000 replicates and matched the archived numeric CSV; leave-one-stratum summaries were also produced. Stage 22 and Stage 24 reproduced the verified interval tables. No overlapping pair was interpreted as its own independent sampling unit. The Stage 26 driver defines stratified endpoint-node weights and resamples the same factor worlds across all compared policy outcomes. Its full inference did not run.

Coverage and abstention use the feasible evaluated pair set for a given setting. Stage 18 measures conditional retention against exact-share certificates at the selected rank. In Guan61 M2, the rank-weighted policy certified 81/222 with QUID and retained 81/83 exact-rank certificates. The outcome-aware within-pair ceiling was 88/222. Stage 24 reports marginal benefits under equal modeled budgets. Costs are synthetic and do not establish deployment economics.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 · SUPPORTED in the pilot setting.** The Guan61 M2 shared-factor scenario gives nonzero certification under incomplete shares.
- **H2 · SUPPORTED in the tested pilot settings.** Guan61 M2 rose from 26.6% at ±10 pp to 36.0% at ±1 pp and 37.4% at exact selected shares. FCID100 SD1 reached 9.0% by ±1 pp in the tested curve. The pattern depends on cohort, policy and starting share precision.
- **H3 · NOT YET TESTED.** No complete observation-aware held-out primary comparison or dependence-aware interval was verified.

Stage 24's small exact subset audit reproduced all four groups. Greedy and exact subsets had identical success counts in the tested groups, with small width differences for some two-factor actions. The coarse versus ±1 pp results are pilot contrasts. The repaired Stage 26 switching calculation pools action counts rather than averaging rates with unlike denominators.
